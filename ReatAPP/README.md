# AURA — Home & Office Architectural Furniture

A luxury furniture platform featuring an 80% rent-to-own equity model and outright purchase showroom.

## Features

- **Rent-to-Own Equity Model**: 80% of monthly rental payments accrue toward outright buyout.
- **Dedicated Listing Sections**:
  - Separate curated Rental section with 3/6/12-month tenure discounts.
  - Dedicated Outright Purchase showroom with price filtering & condition guarantees.
- **Interactive Buyout Calculator**: Dynamic simulator projecting equity accrual and residual buyout balances.
- **Aesthetic Detail Modal**: Full architectural specifications, multi-finish switcher, and rent vs. buy comparisons.
- **Unified Bag Drawer**: Slide-over drawer handling both rental subscriptions and direct purchases.

## Development
